# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Boda boda (motorcycle taxi) riders in Kampala. Phone is mounted on the bike or checked in quick glances in bright outdoor daylight. The job: get spoken, landmark-based directions in Luganda/English without needing to read or parse a map while riding.

## Product Purpose

Ekkubo turns a destination into eyes-free audio navigation: geocode, route, pick real local landmarks (fuel stations, mosques, taxi stages), and speak the instructions. Success means a rider never has to stare at the screen once the route starts.

## Brand Personality

Warm, human, approachable. Built for Kampala riders specifically, not a generic global map product.

## Anti-references

Not a flashy startup landing page: no gradient hero, no glassmorphism, no marketing-site energy. This is a utility a rider glances at before pulling off, not a pitch.

## Design Principles

- Audio-first, screen-second: the UI supports listening, it never competes for attention with the route.
- Legible at a glance in daylight: high contrast, large touch targets, minimal reading required.
- Warm and local, not corporate: feels made for Kampala, not a translated global template.
- Calm confidence: no urgency theatrics, no alarm colors, no anxious motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Primary context is outdoor daylight glare on a mounted or handheld phone, often one-handed. Prioritize large tap targets, high contrast text, and short copy over dense information.
